# Break Escalation Procedure

## Ownership
Every open break has one owner in Settlements Operations from the moment it is detected. The owner investigates,
contacts the counterparty or internal teams, and records each action in the break's history.

## Escalation levels
- Level 1, Operations analyst: investigates and fixes breaks within Operations' own authority.
- Level 2, Operations team lead: needed for any break still open at the end of its first business day, any trade
  over the large-trade threshold, or any break that needs another team to act.
- Level 3, Head of Settlements Operations, with the trading desk: breaks that have failed settlement for two
  business days, or any break where the counterparty disputes the trade terms.

## Timing
Breaks found on trade date should be escalated before the market cut-off if they cannot be fixed in time.
A failed settlement is escalated to Level 2 on the morning after the failure. Failed trades can attract
late-settlement charges from the market, so ageing breaks take priority over new ones.

## Handing over to other teams
- Trading desk or middle office: wrong or disputed economic terms (quantity, price, dates).
- Static Data team: missing, expired or disputed SSIs.
- Counterparty relations: a counterparty that repeatedly fails to confirm or respond.
Record who was contacted, when, and what they agreed, so the next person can pick up the break.

## Closing a break
A break is closed only when the trade has matched or settled, and the cause and fix are recorded. Do not close a
break because a fix has been requested; close it when the fix has worked.
